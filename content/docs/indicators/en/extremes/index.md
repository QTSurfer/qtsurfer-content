In the QTSurfer catalogues, the indicators of counting and extremes are `gain`, `loss`, `max`, `min` and `sum`: they follow a value update by update and keep what a rule often needs, the size of a move, its streak, or what has accumulated since the start. They read an indicator by its name, such as `price`, and work in a `ticker` or a `kline` strategy.

- [Gain](/docs/indicators/extremes/gain): the size of each rise, and how many come in a row.
- [Loss](/docs/indicators/extremes/loss): the size of each fall, and how many come in a row.
- [Maximum](/docs/indicators/extremes/max): the highest value since the start.
- [Minimum](/docs/indicators/extremes/min): the lowest value since the start.
- [Sum](/docs/indicators/extremes/sum): the total since the start.

The catalogues have them with their forms: [QTScript indicators](/docs/strategies/qtscript/indicators) and [Java indicators](/docs/strategies/java/indicators).
