In the QTSurfer catalogues, the bands are `bollinger`, `bollingerPercentB`, `bollingerBandwidth` and the pro bands `donchianChannel`, `envelopes` and `keltnerChannel`: a band drawn around the price, and measures taken from it, where the price sits in the band and how wide the band is.

- [Bollinger bands](/docs/indicators/bands/bollinger): the band, with a chart that reads it.
- [Bollinger %B](/docs/indicators/bands/bollingerPercentB): where the price sits in the band.
- [Bollinger bandwidth](/docs/indicators/bands/bollingerBandwidth): how wide the band is, in percent.
- [Donchian channel](/docs/indicators/bands/donchianChannel): the highest high and the lowest low of the last bars. A pro indicator that reads whole bars.
- [Envelopes](/docs/indicators/bands/envelopes): two lines at a fixed percentage around an exponential average. A pro indicator that reads one price.
- [Keltner channel](/docs/indicators/bands/keltnerChannel): two lines at a multiple of the average true range around an exponential average. A pro indicator that reads whole bars.

The catalogues have them with their forms: [QTScript indicators](/docs/strategies/qtscript/indicators) and [Java indicators](/docs/strategies/java/indicators).
