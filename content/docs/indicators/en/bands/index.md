In the QTSurfer catalogues, the bands are `bollinger`, `bollingerPercentB` and `bollingerBandwidth`: a band drawn around a
moving average of the price, and two measures taken from it, where the price sits in the band and how wide the band is.

- [Bollinger bands](/docs/indicators/bands/bollinger): the band, its %B and its bandwidth.

The catalogues have them with their forms: [QTScript indicators](/docs/strategies/qtscript/indicators) and
[Java indicators](/docs/strategies/java/indicators).
